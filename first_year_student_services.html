Centralized page listing support and services available to first year engineering students.

Welcome! This page helps first year students quickly find the services they need: academic help, labs, counseling, internships, and campus facilities. Each service card gives a brief description and quick actions.

Office Hours:
Mon–Fri • 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Academic Tutoring

Peer tutors and teaching assistants available for basic maths, physics, and programming sessions. Book one-to-one or join group workshops.

Laboratory Assistance

Guidance for experiments, lab safety training, equipment booking, and lab report templates for practicals.

Counseling & Wellbeing

Available counselors for academic stress, time management, and personal support. Confidential appointments on request.

Internships & Placements

Information on internships, skill-building workshops, résumé reviews and campus placement drives targeted for first-year preparation.

Workshops & Seminars

Weekly hands-on workshops: Arduino, basic electronics, CAD, and professional skills (communication, resume writing).

Library & Learning Resources

Access to digital textbooks, past exam papers, and seat reservation for study rooms. Orientation sessions available.
